Ministry of Health and Wellness

Get approval from Environmental Health for food and drink at a temporary event

Use this service if you are organising a temporary event with food or drink, operating a food or drink stall or temporary restaurant at the event, or both.

What you can do

You can use this service to:

  • request an Environmental Health Officer to attend the event
  • apply for a licence to operate a temporary restaurant at the event
  • do both

These are separate processes. Sending a request or application does not mean it has been approved.

Before you start

You will be asked about the event. If you are operating a food or drink stall or temporary restaurant, you will also be asked about your food or drink operation.
